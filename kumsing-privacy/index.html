Kumsing Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 August 2026

App: Kumsing – Manipuri Calendar (com.kanglalabs.kumsing)

Developer: Kangla Labs (kanglalabs@kanglalabs.dev)

Kumsing is built to work offline and to collect as little as possible. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches and why.

What changed on 7 August 2026: Kumsing now shows a small number of advertisements, supplied by Google AdMob. The Advertising section below says exactly where they appear, what the ad network receives, and how to limit it. Nothing else about the app's handling of your data has changed.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own. We do not know how you use the app, and no usage data is sent to us.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos (beyond those you attach yourself), microphone, or SMS access.
  • Nothing you create in Kumsing — your planner events, invitations, tarot journal, rashi or settings — is ever sent to the ad network or used to target advertising.

Advertising

Kumsing shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). They pay for the app's development and keep it free.

Where ads appear. A small banner strip along the bottom edge of the app, below the navigation bar, and one under the filters on the Festivals screen. Never more than one banner on screen at a time. You may also choose to watch a short video ad to unlock a feature for a day; that never starts on its own.

What we will not do with them. No ad is ever placed over, inside or against a reading — the banner is a separate strip at the edge of the window, never mixed into the calendar, the day details, a festival entry, a tarot card or a rashiphal. No full-screen ad ever interrupts you. And no panchang, festival, tatnaba or calendar information is ever placed behind an ad: everything the calendar knows is free, in full, always. The rewarded videos unlock cosmetic and convenience extras only.

What Google receives. To serve and measure ads, Google may access your device's advertising ID, along with technical information such as your device model, operating-system version, coarse region and IP address. This is standard for mobile advertising and is handled under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy — see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Your control.

  • Android → Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation entirely. Kumsing respects that choice; ads still appear, but are not personalised.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Kumsing asks for your consent choices the first time it runs, using Google's consent form, and honours the answer.

We do not operate an ad network, we do not run our own ad targeting, and we never hand your Kumsing content to an advertiser.

Data stored only on your device

Everything you create in Kumsing lives in a private database on your phone: planner events, invitation cards and their photos, tarot journal entries, your chosen rashi, themes and settings. If you never sign in, nothing ever leaves your device.

Phone calendar import (optional): if you use "Import events" and grant the calendar permission, Kumsing reads your device calendar once to copy the events you select into its own planner. It never modifies or deletes anything in your phone calendar, and the permission is used for nothing else.

Data stored in the cloud (only if you sign in)

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, asia-south2/Delhi region) under your account, solely to back up your data and sync it between your devices:

  • Your Google account's basic profile (name, email) — used to show who is signed in and to identify your backup.
  • Your planner events, invitation entries (without photos), tarot journal entries and chosen rashi.

This data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), is readable only by your authenticated account under Firebase security rules, and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling. Apart from Google Firebase (backup and sign-in) and Google AdMob (advertising, described above), we use no other third-party services.

Delete your account and data

This section explains how to delete your Kumsing account (developer: Kangla Labs) and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion — two ways:

  1. In the app (immediate): open Kumsing → More → Settings → Account → "Delete account & cloud data", then confirm. This deletes your account and cloud data straight away.
  2. By email: write to kanglalabs@kanglalabs.dev from the email address you signed in with, asking to delete your account. We complete these requests within 30 days.

What is deleted: your Kumsing (Firebase) account and all of the cloud-backed data stored under it — your name and email taken from Google sign-in, and your synced planner events, invitation entries, tarot journal entries and chosen rashi. This removal is permanent and the data is not retained after deletion (server backups holding it roll off within 30 days).

What is kept: nothing on our servers. Any data stored only on your phone (the on-device copy of your planner, invitations, tarot notes and settings) stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall Kumsing — it is never on our servers to begin with.

Signing out is different from deletion: it only stops syncing and leaves your data on your device and in your cloud backup.

Children

Kumsing is a general-audience calendar app and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
